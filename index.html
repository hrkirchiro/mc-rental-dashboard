How to add your units

Edit the MY_UNITS array in index.html with your property details (name, neighborhood, beds, baths, sq ft, your rent, notes). The dashboard will automatically compare your rents against Zillow market averages for each neighborhood and bedroom type. Units within ±3% of market are flagged as At market, above as Above market, and below as Below market.
